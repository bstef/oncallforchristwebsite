Ambassadors for Christ.

A ministry focused on the Gospel, the youth of the Rosebud Reservation, and the training of believers for global service.

Our Mission

"United to share the Gospel with the lost, to disciple followers of Christ, and to train believers in living a Gospel-centered life. By the Lord's grace, we seek to sustain a committed presence on the Rosebud Indian Reservation, to continue to develop our relationships with the youth of Okreek and Parmelee communities..."

Join Our Mission

Romania: The Heritage

Romania serves as the spiritual and training foundation for our ministry. With its deep history and complex spiritual landscape, it is the ideal environment to equip ambassadors for Christ.

We are committed to training our team members here in Gospel-focused ministry, ensuring they are prepared to use their gifts for the glory of God anywhere in the world.

Our Focus in Romania

  • • Theological & Practical Training
  • • Equipping Local Believers
  • • Gospel-Centered Leadership

South Dakota: The Presence

Rosebud Reservation

We maintain a committed presence in the communities of **Okreek** and **Parmelee**. Our work isn't about short-term visits; it's about life-on-life ministry.

Youth & Families

By developing deep relationships with the youth, we open doors to reach their families. Our intention is to see people incorporated into a vibrant fellowship of believers through repentance and faith in Him.
