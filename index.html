United to Share the Gospel

By the Lord's grace, we seek to be ambassadors for Christ, calling people to repentance and faith in Him across cultures and communities.

Our Mission

United to share the Gospel with the lost, to disciple followers of Christ, and to train believers in living a Gospel-centered life.

By the Lord's grace, we seek to sustain a committed presence on the Rosebud Indian Reservation, to continue to develop our relationships with the youth of Okreek and Parmelee communities, and to reach out to their families with the Gospel. Our intention is to be ambassadors for Christ, calling people to repentance and faith in Him, and to see them incorporated into a fellowship of believers.

We are also committed to training the members of our team in Gospel-focused ministry, equipping them for service and enabling them to use their gifts for the glory of God and the furtherance of His Kingdom.

South Dakota Native American Ministry

The Rosebud Indian Reservation

The Rosebud Indian Reservation is home to the Sicangu Lakota (Burnt Thigh) people, located in south-central South Dakota. This reservation faces significant challenges including poverty, substance abuse, and limited economic opportunities, yet it is also a place of rich cultural heritage and resilient people.

Our Presence in Okreek and Parmelee

We have established a sustained ministry presence in the communities of Okreek and Parmelee, two small towns on the reservation. Our focus is on building genuine, long-term relationships with the youth and their families, demonstrating the love of Christ through consistent presence and authentic care.

Ministry Focus Areas

Our work in South Dakota encompasses several key areas:

  • Youth ministry programs that provide mentorship, discipleship, and positive activities
  • Family outreach that brings the Gospel message to entire households
  • Community development initiatives that address both spiritual and practical needs
  • Cultural sensitivity training for our team members to serve with respect and understanding
  • Partnership with local believers to establish sustainable, indigenous-led fellowships

The Need

The spiritual need on the Rosebud Reservation is profound. Many young people are searching for hope, purpose, and identity. Our calling is to introduce them to Jesus Christ, who offers abundant life, and to see them grow as His disciples within the context of their own community and culture.

Romania Ministry

About Romania

Romania is a nation in Southeast Europe with a rich Christian heritage dating back centuries. Following decades of communist rule that suppressed religious freedom, Romania has experienced a spiritual awakening with growing evangelical movements and a hunger for authentic biblical teaching.

Our Ministry Approach

Our work in Romania focuses on coming alongside local believers to strengthen and expand Gospel ministry throughout the country. We believe in empowering Romanian Christians to lead their own ministries while providing support, training, and partnership.

Key Ministry Activities

Our Romania ministry includes:

  • Evangelistic outreach in communities with limited Gospel witness
  • Training seminars for pastors and church leaders in biblical teaching and discipleship
  • Support for church planting efforts in both urban and rural areas
  • Youth camps and programs that introduce young people to Christ
  • Equipping believers for Gospel-centered living and effective witness
  • Developing indigenous ministry leaders who can continue the work long-term

The Opportunity

Romania represents a unique opportunity for Gospel ministry. The people are open to spiritual discussions, there is a growing hunger for biblical truth, and local believers are passionate about reaching their own nation. Our role is to encourage, equip, and partner with these dedicated Romanian Christians as they advance God's Kingdom.

Cultural Context

Understanding Romanian culture, history, and the legacy of communism is essential to effective ministry. We invest time in cultural learning and building deep relationships that allow for authentic Gospel sharing within the Romanian context.
