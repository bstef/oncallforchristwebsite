ROMANIA & SOUTH DAKOTA

AMBASSADORS
FOR CHRIST

"United to share the Gospel with the lost, to disciple followers of Christ, and to train believers in living a Gospel-centered life. Our intention is to be ambassadors for Christ, calling people to repentance and faith in Him."

01. MISSION

Developing relationships with the youth of Okreek and Parmelee to reach their families with the Gospel.

02. TRAINING

Equipping the team for service and enabling them to use their gifts for the glory of God.

THE ROMANIA HERITAGE

Our ministry is rooted in the training and discipline of Gospel-centered service found in Romania. Here, we equip our team to be sent into the world.

Preparation

Focusing on rigorous Gospel study and practical training to sustain a committed presence in cross-cultural fields.

THE ROSEBUD PRESENCE

Focusing on the communities of Okreek and Parmelee, we are building a foundation of faith on the Rosebud Indian Reservation.

Okreek Outreach

Youth-focused ministry designed to provide a stable, Christ-centered presence in the community.

Parmelee Growth

Building deep relationships with families and incorporating them into a local fellowship of believers.
