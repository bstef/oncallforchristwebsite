# On Call For Christ - Mission Ministry Website

A modern, responsive website for On Call For Christ, a Gospel-focused mission ministry serving communities in South Dakota and Romania.

## About

On Call For Christ is dedicated to sharing the Gospel with the lost, discipling followers of Christ, and training believers in living a Gospel-centered life. This website showcases our mission work and provides information about our ministry fields.

## Mission Statement

United to share the Gospel with the lost, to disciple followers of Christ, and to train believers in living a Gospel-centered life.

By the Lord's grace, we seek to sustain a committed presence on the Rosebud Indian Reservation, to continue to develop our relationships with the youth of Okreek and Parmelee communities, and to reach out to their families with the Gospel. Our intention is to be ambassadors for Christ, calling people to repentance and faith in Him, and to see them incorporated into a fellowship of believers.

We are also committed to training the members of our team in Gospel-focused ministry, equipping them for service and enabling them to use their gifts for the glory of God and the furtherance of His Kingdom.

## Mission Fields

### South Dakota Native American Ministry
Our ministry maintains a committed presence on the Rosebud Indian Reservation, specifically serving the Lakota people in the Okreek and Parmelee communities. We focus on:
- Youth ministry and mentorship
- Family outreach programs
- Community development
- Building long-term relationships
- Supporting indigenous-led fellowships

### Romania Ministry
Partnering with local believers throughout Romania to:
- Conduct evangelistic outreach
- Provide pastoral training and discipleship
- Support church planting efforts
- Equip believers for Gospel-centered living
- Develop indigenous ministry leaders

## Deployment

This site is designed to be deployed using GitHub Pages:

1. Upload the `index.html` file to your repository
2. Go to Settings → Pages
3. Select "Deploy from a branch"
4. Choose "main" branch and "/ (root)" folder
5. Click Save

Your site will be live at: `https://[username].github.io/[repository-name]/`
